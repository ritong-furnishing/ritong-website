Hotel Project

Grand Hyatt Hotel

Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
Project Overview

Full FF&E delivery for guest rooms and public areas in Kuala Lumpur.

RITONG delivered custom furniture and joinery for the Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur, covering guest rooms and public areas. The project demonstrates our capability to execute large-scale hospitality developments across Southeast Asia, coordinating design, production and delivery to meet international brand standards.

Scope of Work

Furniture

Custom beds, seating, tables and case goods for guest rooms and public areas.

Joinery & Millwork

Wardrobes, vanity units, wall panelling and architectural millwork.

Coordination

Design coordination, production management, quality control and logistics.
