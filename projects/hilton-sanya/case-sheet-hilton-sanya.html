Hilton Sanya Huixin Road

Sanya, China
Project Overview

280-room Hilton Garden Inn with comprehensive wood finishes and joinery.

The Hilton Garden Inn Sanya Huixin Road is a 280-room hospitality development. RITONG produced and installed wood finishes, fixtures, doors and public area joinery — delivering the full scope of interior millwork and fitted elements required for a Hilton-branded property.

Scope of Work

Furniture

Custom beds, seating, desks and case goods for 280 guest rooms.

Joinery & Millwork

Wood finishes, doors, wall panelling and public area millwork.

Coordination

Production oversight, quality control and delivery coordination.
