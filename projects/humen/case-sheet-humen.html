The Project

This private mansion in Humen, Dongguan is built on the bones of French Baroque with the grace of Rococo — gilded hand-carving, tiffany-blue lacquered surfaces and hand-painted chinoiserie wall panels carry the splendour of a European court into a contemporary home. ritong delivered the project as a full turnkey package: bespoke furniture, wall panelling and complete soft furnishing across six spaces — entry, foyer, sitting room, master bedroom, dressing area and a daughter's suite — tied together by one consistent language of gold, velvet, marble and vened glass.

The owners hold a lifelong love for European classical art and asked for a home that feels as ceremonial as a palace yet as warm and functional as a family house. Guided by the principle of "unified yet never repeated", the design team kept the core materials and palette consistent while giving each space its own character through furniture silhouette, fabric pattern and lighting layering.

01

The EntryTiffany-Blue Gilded Console

A tiffany-blue lacquered console with full hand-gilded carving sets the baroque tone of the entire mansion.

The entry is the opening line of the house. At 1.4 metres, the console is finished in tiffany-blue lacquer beneath full hand-gilded carving — intertwining lotus scrolls, acanthus and mythical beasts stacked in layers that echo eighteenth-century French court furniture. The top is Greek-imported Calacatta marble, a cool counterpoint to the blue-and-gold facade. Above it, a pair of cloisonné vases and a spray of Rococo dried florals set the first visual anchor for every guest.

Console — ritong in-house workshop · 47-stage gilding process

02

The FoyerRomance of Black & Gold

Black lacquer and gold leaf beside a classical oil painting turn every passage into a ritual.

From the entry, a roughly six-metre foyer is a moment of transition. Both walls are clad in black lacquer with gilded panelling, the gold motifs painted by hand on site — every curve demanding three layers of leaf and five of varnish. The floor is dark-grey marble parquet, a calm weight against the black-and-gold walls. At the far end, an eighteenth-century-style portrait hangs in a gilded frame, establishing the artistic key of the whole house.

Panelling — 24k gold leaf · hand-painted on site · 47 days

03

The Master BedroomSleeping in a Gilded Reverie

A fully gilded carved headboard, a deep-green velvet bench and a crystal chandelier compose a three-dimensional French dream.

The master bedroom concentrates the most craft in the house. The headboard is a fully gilded carved work, hand-finished by three carvers over ninety days. The backdrop wall uses cream panelling; its linework draws on Greek acanthus, paired with 1.5-centimetre plaster mouldings and concealed LED strips for a soft layered glow. Bedding in deep-green velvet meets the gold of the headboard in a classic court palette; the crystal chandelier is bespoke, after a nineteenth-century French model, hand-strung with 12,000 Swarovski crystals.

Headboard — solid-wood hand carving · 24k gold-leaf veneer

04

The DressingCraft in Every Curve

A fully gilded dressing table is the close-up of the craft.

The dressing table is the close-up of the master suite. Every visible face — facade, drawers, mirror frame, legs — is fully gilded carving. The mirror's back panel and frame build gold through five stages — carving, lacquering, leafing, burnishing, top-coating — doubling the bedroom in reflection. The tri-fold mirror adjusts to the moment; a green-velvet stool echoes the bedding. The marble top is inlaid as a single slab, joints held within 0.3 millimetres, consistent with the house-wide standard.

Detail — solid-wood hand carving · 8-coat gilding · joints ≤0.3mm

05

The Sitting RoomLight, Marble & Gilded Lines

White gilded panelling, a blue floral sofa and marble floors let receiving guests and family time feel equally at ease.

The sitting room is the family core of the house. Walls are mostly white gilded panelling; clean gold lines organise the geometry, a rhythmic contrast to the fully gilded bedroom. A cream-gold three-seater and a blue floral armchair enclose a conversational group; a gilded side table holds the owners' collection of blue-and-white porcelain and bronze objects, carrying family memory. The floor is Italian-imported Calacatta marble parquet with naturally continuous veining and no visible seams across the room.

Sofa & Chairs — ritong in-house soft-furnishing · solid frame · goose-down fill

06

The Daughter's RoomA Softer French Note

The gentler side of French style — a soft powder-blue floral language made for the home's young daughter.

The daughter's room continues the French vocabulary but in a softer powder-blue floral key, answering the personality of the young family member. Bedding, drapery and armchair use French-imported floral fabric that picks up the pale-blue panelled wall. The wall line switches from gold to silver leaf — equally fine yet lighter — the most tender corner of the whole mansion.

Soft Furnishings — French-imported floral fabric · silver-leaf detailing
