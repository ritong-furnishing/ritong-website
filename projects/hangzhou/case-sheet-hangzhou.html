The Project

This private residence in Hangzhou's Rose Garden is a study in French neo-classical restraint. White-painted panelling with gilt linework, powder-blue silk upholstery and parquet floors form a quiet, refined stage for the family's daily life. ritong delivered the project as a full turnkey package — bespoke furniture, fixed millwork and complete soft furnishing across sitting room, drawing room, dining room, master bedroom, study and guest bedroom — anchored by a single, consistent language of painted panelling, gold detailing and natural light.

The owners asked for a home that reads as "a Parisian apartment above the gardens", warm enough for a family with school-age children, refined enough for formal entertaining. The design team answered with a pared-back French vocabulary — no heavy gilding, no fussy carving — letting proportion, fabric and joinery carry the elegance. Each space shares the same panelling geometry and warm-white palette, while the furniture silhouettes and upholstery shift gently to give every room its own character.

01

The Drawing RoomA Quieter Second Living

White-painted panelling and a powder-blue silk sofa open the room to the garden through tall French windows.

Across the entrance hall, the drawing room is the family's quieter second living. Walls are entirely in white-painted panelling, the gold detailing kept to a single line around each frame so the geometry does the work. A powder-blue silk three-seater and a pair of matching armchairs face a low marble coffee table; a long console along the inner wall is a bespoke piece, its top in a single Calacatta slab, its front hand-painted to match the wall panelling. Tall French windows along the garden side let the room read as a continuation of the outdoors in summer.

Panelling — hand-painted in ritong workshop · Calacatta marble, single slab

02

The Dining RoomClassical Geometry, Modern Calm

A round dining table beneath a crystal chandelier, set on hand-laid oak parquet in a classical fish-bone pattern.

The formal dining room centres on a round oak table that seats eight, surrounded by hand-carved chairs in a soft celadon-green silk. The crystal chandelier above is bespoke — a quiet nod to the eighteenth-century French models the owners had collected on their travels, redrawn in a lighter silhouette. The floor is hand-laid oak parquet in a classical fish-bone pattern, a foundational decision that quietly ties the entire ground floor together. A pair of hand-painted display cabinets flank the entrance, holding the family's collection of celadon and famille-rose porcelain.

Dining table & chairs — ritong bespoke · oak parquet, hand-laid

03

The Master BedroomSunlit, Soft, Considered

White panelling, an upholstered headboard and a deep bay window give the master bedroom its quiet, sunlit weight.

The master bedroom takes the same restrained French vocabulary and softens it for sleep. The walls are white-painted panelling, the headboard a tall, deep-buttoned upholstered piece in ivory linen. A deep bay window with built-in bench serves as a reading nook by day and a quiet retreat at night. Bedding is in cream silk with a thin celadon stripe; curtains in unlined linen filter the morning light without blocking it. The dressing area continues the same language, with hand-painted wardrobes and a vanity table topped in a single marble slab.

Headboard & panelling — hand-painted · bespoke dressing table, single-slab marble

04

The StudyA Library Calm

A round writing table, a deep-buttoned reading chair and a wall of bespoke bookshelves give the study its library calm.

The study is the most private room in the house. A round walnut writing table sits in the centre, lit by a small brass library lamp; a deep-buttoned reading chair in chestnut leather faces the window. One entire wall is lined with bespoke bookshelves in white-painted wood, with a rolling library ladder on a brass rail. The panelling pattern continues from the main rooms but in a quieter rhythm, and the parquet narrows to a smaller scale to suit the room's proportions. A hidden door leads to the master suite, allowing the study to read as either a private retreat or an extension of the bedroom.

Bookcases — ritong bespoke, white-painted · rolling ladder, brass rail

05

The Guest BedroomWarm Welcome in Blue

Powder-blue silk, a hand-painted headboard and a window seat keep the guest bedroom both welcoming and considered.

The guest bedroom is the warmest expression of the home's blue accent. Walls are panelled in the same white-painted geometry, with a single tall window and a built-in window seat below. The headboard is hand-painted in powder-blue, framed in a thin gilt line that echoes the panelling. Bedding is in powder-blue silk, with a single cream coverlet folded at the foot. A small writing desk and a slim wardrobe complete the room — sized for an extended-stay guest, but with the same craft and material language as the rest of the house.

Headboard — hand-painted in ritong workshop · powder-blue silk bedding

06

The Sitting RoomParisian Salon, Family Scale

The chandelier lowers to reading height; powder-blue silk catches the afternoon light through the long windows.

The main sitting room reads as a classical Parisian salon, scaled for a Chinese family. A pair of teal silk sofas face each other across a low marble coffee table, flanked by a pair of hand-painted armchairs in ivory. The wall behind the sofas is the room's quiet showpiece: full-height hand-painted panelling in soft celadon, with a central breakfront holding a bespoke bar cabinet and a pair of gilt-framed mirrors. The chandelier — a bespoke design in cut crystal and brushed brass — can be lowered to reading height on a concealed winch. Afternoon light from the long windows is the room's most reliable visitor.

Chandelier — bespoke design, cut crystal · bar cabinet, hand-painted
