509 bespoke apartments in a Singaporean landmark by Moshe Safdie — ritong delivered the full FF&E package, every unit to a single standard.

The Project

Sky Habitat is Moshe Safdie’s 509-unit residential landmark in Singapore, its stepped, interlocking balconies a city signature. ritong delivered the complete apartment FF&E package — custom kitchens, wardrobes, vanity units, shoe cabinets and bathroom joinery — across all 509 units, with a coordinated finish schedule that gave every apartment a consistent feel while leaving room for unit-level variation. The work was delivered to a developer’s programme that demanded both scale and craft.
