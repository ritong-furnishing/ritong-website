What We Deliver

Furniture · Joinery · Lighting · Rugs · Curtains · Wallcoverings · Artwork & Accessories

We work alongside owners, designers and project teams to bring the agreed design into the finished space — coordinating every element from bespoke furniture production to final installation and styling.

01

Bespoke Furniture & Joinery

We turn project requirements and design specifications into bespoke furniture and joinery. From dimensions and proportions to finishes and construction details, each piece is developed for its place within the overall scheme, supported by technical drawings and sample approval.

02

Complete Furnishing Solutions

Alongside bespoke furniture, we select and source lighting, rugs, curtains, wallcoverings, artwork and accessories. Working to the project's design direction, we coordinate these elements across rooms and shared areas to create a cohesive, fully furnished interior.

03

Project Management & Delivery

We coordinate production, quality checks, export packing and international logistics, then work with the site team on installation and final styling. Each stage is managed around the agreed specifications, delivery schedule and site requirements.
