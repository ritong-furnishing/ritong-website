Brand Story

From Vision to Finished Space.

RITONG brings bespoke furniture and complete FF&E solutions to private residences, hotels, apartments and commercial spaces worldwide.

Founded in 2004 with lampshades, lighting and decorative accessories, we expanded into furniture in 2006. Today, our work connects scheme development, custom manufacturing and furnishing coordination with international delivery, installation coordination and final styling.

Rooted in Craft. Working Across Styles.

French classical craftsmanship remains a defining strength. Our capabilities extend across contemporary, modern luxury and Art Deco interiors, shaped around each project's design direction and practical requirements.

Our Journey

Built Over Time.

2004
Our Beginning. Established Final Touch, specialising in lampshades, lighting and decorative accessories.
2006
Into Furniture. Established Monica Shade & Lamps Home Design Company and expanded into furniture import and export.
2007
Manzel Collection. Introduced our French classical furniture brand, shaping a lasting focus on craftsmanship.
2008
Manufacturing & the Middle East. Established our first classical solid-wood furniture factory and entered the Middle East through R&B Interior.
2011
Mansion Interior. Extended our capabilities into interior design, furnishing schemes and colour coordination.
2017
Richard Living. Introduced a contemporary American luxury collection, broadening our design portfolio.
2020
Art Deco. Expanded our contemporary offering through a collection connecting classical influences with modern design.
2026 — Present
The Story Continues. Today, RITONG brings together bespoke furniture, lighting and furnishings for residences, hotels and commercial spaces worldwide, from design development to final styling.
